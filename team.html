A nation of rich heritage,
a medicine of many voices.

Oman's healthcare is shaped by centuries of trade, tradition, and a mosaic of Arabic dialects. Every governorate has its own way of describing pain, illness, and healing. Medad is trained on this country — not generic Arabic from elsewhere.
