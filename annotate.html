Quality Assurance

Two-Reviewer System

Every clip is reviewed by at least two independent annotators. If they disagree, a third senior reviewer resolves the conflict.

R1

First Review

المراجعة الأولى

You listen and correct the auto-transcription. Your version is saved.

R2

Second Review

المراجعة الثانية

Another annotator independently reviews the same clip. Agreement is measured.

R3

Conflict Resolution

حل الخلافات

If agreement is below 75%, a third reviewer makes the final decision.

FAQ

Common Questions

كيف أسجل؟
How do I register?
Visit the annotation platform and enter your email. New users will be asked to fill in their name, country, and dialect. An admin will review and approve your account — you'll receive an email when approved.
هل هناك اعتراف بالمساهمة؟
Will my contribution be recognized?
Yes. Active annotators are tracked on a live leaderboard. Top contributors may be acknowledged in research publications, and eligible annotators receive compensation for their work. Your "My Stats" dashboard tracks all your progress in real-time.
هل أحتاج خلفية طبية؟
Do I need a medical background?
Not necessarily. Many clips are general Arabic conversations. However, medical professionals (doctors, nurses, pharmacists) are especially valuable for correcting clinical terminology.
كم من الوقت يستغرق كل مقطع؟
How long does each clip take?
Most clips are 10-30 seconds of audio. A single annotation takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes depending on whether the text needs corrections. The platform tracks your time automatically.
هل بياناتي آمنة؟
Is my data safe?
Yes. The annotation platform runs on secure servers. Clinical audio never leaves our infrastructure. Your personal information is only used for account management and reward processing.
