Become an Annotator

How review tiers work

All new applicants begin as External Reviewers. You'll handle first-pass (R1) and second-pass (R2) reviews — listening to clips, correcting the AI's draft, tagging dialect and medical terms. Same pay rate as everyone else.

After consistent high-quality work, you may be invited to join the internal Core Team — a small group of trusted reviewers who break ties when two annotators disagree (R3). This protects the final quality of the training dataset and is an honour, not an entry-level role.

Annotation Platform

Where annotators do their work

Our annotation platform is a bilingual (Arabic/English) web tool where annotators listen to audio clips, correct AI transcriptions, tag dialects and medical terms. Each clip is reviewed by two independent annotators for quality assurance.
