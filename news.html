Major Launch March 2026

Arabic Medical Speech Corpus Builder — Now Live

We are excited to announce the launch of the Arabic Medical Speech Corpus Builder — a first-of-its-kind platform for building structured, high-quality Arabic medical speech datasets. The tool enables clinicians to record, transcribe, validate, and annotate medical conversations in Gulf Arabic dialects, creating the foundational dataset that will power Medad's AI engine.

The platform features an intuitive web interface for managing recording sessions, real-time transcription review, medical terminology tagging, and quality scoring — all running 100% locally to ensure complete data sovereignty. Currently managed and developed with AI-assisted engineering, this project represents a major step toward building the largest Arabic clinical speech corpus ever assembled.

Milestone March 2026

Data Collection Has Begun

Clinical data collection is now underway across participating hospitals in Muscat. Our team is actively recording real-world doctor-patient conversations in Gulf Arabic, building the foundation for Medad's speech recognition and clinical note generation models.

Technical March 2026

Model Training Initiated

Initial model training has kicked off using collected speech data. We are fine-tuning Arabic ASR models and local LLMs on Omani medical terminology, Gulf dialect patterns, and clinical note structures — all processed on our on-premise GPU infrastructure.

Team February 2025

Medad Research Team Formed

A multidisciplinary team of 11 clinicians, researchers, and engineers from across Oman's top medical institutions has come together to build Medad — the first Arabic ambient clinical documentation system.
